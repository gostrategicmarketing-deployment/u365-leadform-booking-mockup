How a lead gets here

  1. He taps a Sean video ad on Facebook or Instagram and opens the lead form.
  2. He answers 3 questions. "No" or "Not yet" ends on the $97 Protocol screen, and he never reaches this page.
  3. If he qualifies, the form's last screen says "You qualify. Now pick your time." and the Pick my time button opens this page.
  4. He books on the existing consultation calendar. Booking stops every lead-form follow-up and starts the approved pre-call texts and emails.
  5. If he doesn't book, Alejandro is already alerted to call him, and the approved follow-up runs.
